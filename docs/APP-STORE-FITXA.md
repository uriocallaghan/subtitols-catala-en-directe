# Fitxa de l'App Store de duna

Aquests són els textos per omplir la fitxa a App Store Connect, a punt per copiar. Els límits de caràcters són els d'Apple i tots es compleixen.

Totes les frases descriuen el que l'app fa avui. Cap no la presenta com un aparell mèdic o auditiu (norma 1.4.1).

**Idiomes de la fitxa.** App Store Connect no ofereix gallec ni basc com a idioma de fitxa. La fitxa va en tres idiomes:
- anglès (English (U.S.)), com a idioma principal: és el que veuen els països sense cap dels altres;
- català;
- castellà (Spanish (Spain)).

**Nom.** "duna" sol ja està agafat a l'App Store: hi ha una app "Duna" (Copesa) i una "DUNA" (ORG DIKIDI), i Apple no deixa repetir noms. Per això el nom de la fitxa porta una coletilla. Sota la icona del telèfon i del Mac sempre es llegirà només "duna". En anglès no fem servir "Live Captions", que és el nom d'una funció d'Apple.

## English (U.S.): idioma principal

**Name** (30): `duna: live transcription`

**Subtitle** (30): `Captions on your device`

**Promotional text** (170; es pot canviar sense revisió):
> Read what is being said around you, live, in English, Spanish, Catalan, Galician and Basque. After a one-time language download it works offline, on your iPhone or Mac.

**Description:**
> duna turns speech into text as people talk. Put your iPhone or Mac nearby, tap (or click) the screen and read.
>
> MADE FOR READING
> • Text rolls up line by line and holds still: words you have already read do not move.
> • Punctuation, capitals and numbers as digits, added as people speak.
> • When a different person starts speaking, a new paragraph usually begins.
> • Large type you can resize, and four typefaces, two of them designed for low vision (Atkinson Hyperlegible).
> • Light and dark mode.
>
> PRIVATE, FOR REAL
> • Everything happens on your iPhone or Mac. No audio or text is sent anywhere.
> • After a one-time language download, it works offline, in Airplane Mode too.
> • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Free.
>
> MORE THAN LIVE
> • Make subtitles for a video or recording you already have: duna writes an .srt file on the device.
> • Export the transcript as text, Markdown or .srt subtitles.
> • Shortcuts actions, and a Control Center button on iPhone.
>
> LANGUAGES
> • English, Spanish, Catalan, Galician and Basque. Galician and Basque are the newest and make more mistakes.
> • The app stays small: pick a language and download it once (120–190 MB).
> • The app itself speaks all five languages.
>
> duna is an automatic transcription and can make mistakes. It is not a hearing aid and does not replace professional captioning.

**Keywords** (100): `transcribe,speech to text,subtitles,srt,deaf,hard of hearing,accessibility,lecture,meeting,notes`

> FALLBACK FOR THE DOWNLOAD (new in this build, after the previous rejection). >
> HOW TO TEST (iPhone and Mac)

> 5. Share icon (top right): export .txt, .md or .srt. Gallery icon (top left; on Mac also File > Subtitles for a Video…, ⌘O): make .srt subtitles for a video.
