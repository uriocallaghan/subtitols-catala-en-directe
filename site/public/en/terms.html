What the app does

duna turns what the microphone hears into text, live, and makes subtitles for the videos and recordings you give it. All of it happens on your device. The languages (Catalan, Spanish, English, Galician and Basque) are downloaded one by one from Apple's servers when you ask for them; the app shows their size before downloading.

Free

duna is free. It has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads and no accounts.

Automatic transcription makes mistakes

duna is an automatic transcription, and it gets things wrong. It can mishear words, names, numbers or punctuation, and does so more often with background noise, with several people talking at once, far from the microphone, or with unusual accents and vocabulary. Galician and Basque are marked New: they are the most recent and make more mistakes still.

duna is not a medical device or a hearing aid, and it does not replace a professional captioning or interpreting service, or any prescribed assistive device. Do not rely on it where a mistake could have serious consequences: emergencies, safety announcements, medical or legal information, or any situation in which you need an exact transcript. In those cases, always confirm by another means.
